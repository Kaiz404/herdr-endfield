## Fonts

**Saira**, as `skin/mods/endfield/fonts/EndfieldWide.ttf`.
Copyright 2020 The Saira Project Authors (https://github.com/Omnibus-Type/Saira).
Licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1; the full licence is in `skin/mods/endfield/fonts/OFL.txt`.
The file is a static instance of `Saira[wdth,wght].ttf` from the google/fonts repository (`ofl/saira/`) at width 125 and weight 600, renamed to the family "Endfield Wide". Saira declares no Reserved Font Name. `skin/mods/endfield/tools/instance_font.py` rebuilds it.

JetBrains Mono, Geist and Geist Mono are used by name. They ship with Herdr and are not included here.

## Icons and art

Every SVG in `skin/mods/endfield/art/` and `skin/layouts/endfield.svg` is original to this project. The icons are generated by `skin/mods/endfield/tools/make_art.py`; the layout preview is drawn by hand. `preview.png` is `skin/layouts/endfield.svg` rendered at 4x.

No character art is included, and this repository distributes no official art. The optional art pack (`art-pack/`) is built on each person's machine, from images they supply or with `scripts/fetch-operators.mjs`. That script downloads the official renders from the official site directly onto the person's machine, for their personal use, after showing a notice and asking for consent. `art-pack/operators.json` holds only those files' URLs, sha256 hashes and crop settings.
